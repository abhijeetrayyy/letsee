# Movie/TV Tracking, Journaling & Social Apps — Competitive Landscape Research
Date: 2026-09-10 | Status: COMPLETE (≈75 web searches + ≈60 page fetches)

## Method and limits
- Sources: vendor sites/docs, Trakt forums, Trakt public roadmap, App Store/Trustpilot/JustUseApp reviews, MacRumors/ResetEra/Apple Community/IMDb Community threads, Product Hunt, TechCrunch/Variety/SCMP/etc., and comparison blogs (Achriom, Moviebase, Hobi, Cinopsys) that quote Reddit.
- **Reddit itself was not reachable** (reddit.com blocks the crawler and is blocked at browser policy level). Every Reddit thread topic requested ("trakt vs letterboxd", "tv time alternative", "app to decide what to watch with friends", etc.) was covered via the equivalent forum/store/blog discussions above, and via blogs that summarize Reddit sentiment. Treat "Reddit says…" claims as second-hand.
- The single biggest 2026 event: **TV Time (25M registered users) shut down July 15, 2026** and deleted all data. Most of the "what people want" evidence below comes from the refugee wave that followed.

## 2. Deep dives

### 2.1 Trakt
Trakt is the plumbing of the category. Its public API and scrobbling integrations (Plex, Emby, Kodi, Jellyfin, Infuse) mean most third-party trackers (Showly, Hobi, Moviebase, Sofa Time, CinExplore, Cinopsys) are effectively Trakt clients, and the community consensus after TV Time died was "Get your data into Trakt first, then pick the app you enjoy opening" (https://moviebase.app/resources/tv-time-alternatives). It claims 14M+ users (https://www.cbinsights.com/company/trakt/). Its strengths are exactly what a passive logger needs: episode-level history with multiple plays, calendars, collections, personal notes, and — since Nov 2024 — importers for IMDb and Letterboxd (https://alternativeto.net/news/2024/11/trakt-introduces-importer-for-imdb-and-letterboxd), plus a TV Time importer (~95% accuracy, but episode ratings are dropped and TheTVDB→TMDB re-matching is "occasionally messy" per https://cinopsysapp.com/compare/tv-time-vs-trakt and https://hobiapp.com/blog/best-tv-show-tracker-apps).

2025 was Trakt's annus horribilis with users. In February it capped the free tier (2 personal lists, 100 items per list, 100 watchlist items, 100 collection items, 100 notes) and doubled VIP from $30 to $60/yr while adding collaborative lists, a universal "Watch now" filter and notes for free (https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/2/trakt-tv-has-set-stricter-limits-for-free-users-and-raised-vip-subscription-prices-by-100-/). In May it broke the co-founder's promise to honor legacy $15/$30 plans, forcing all renewals to $60 from May 20/June 12 — up to a 300% hike — with staff admitting an internal vote against it (https://www.neowin.net/news/trakt-vip-receives-up-to-300-price-hike-going-back-on-promise-to-honor-legacy-subs/ ; https://forums.trakt.tv/t/upcoming-vip-renewal-pricing-changes-effective-june-12-2025/56649). Trustpilot is 1.5/5 with 89% one-star: "Trakt has become way too money-hungry, and it feels like more and more features are being locked behind a paywall"; "The V3 version of the site on desktop is awful"; scrobbling was restricted to one connected app; users report support silence and forum posts deleted for criticism (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trakt.tv).

The V3 redesign compounded it. Forum feedback: "the progress page … that is most essential to me" is gone; "Notes are gone. I use these to track quotes"; "Star ratings: Bring back out of 10"; "I can't find my lists. I'm only seeing 4 lists, when I probably have close to 100 … Lists are my sole reason for VIP"; screenshots replaced posters so users "have no way to correlate the images with the shows"; "I can't add a TV show or movie watch date from the past anymore" (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/new-trakt-feedback/84794?page=11). App Store reviews echo: 10-star ratings downgraded to 5, per-season ratings lost, daily watch-time stats removed, "The price doubled" while features were "nerfed" (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trakt-tv-shows-movies/id1514873602?see-all=reviews). The public roadmap's top asks are restorations: RSS/iCal/CSV feeds, full history/stats views, progress percentages, seasons/episodes in lists, marking future episodes watched (https://roadmap.trakt.tv/en).

Automatic tracking from streaming apps — the thing everyone wants — arrived Dec 2024 as the VIP-only "Streaming Scrobbler" (Netflix, Prime, Hulu, Apple TV, later Disney+, Max, Paramount+), syncing ~10 minutes after linking and then every 24 hours (https://alternativeto.net/news/2024/12/trakt-tv-launches-streaming-scrobbler-to-sync-viewing-history-across-streaming-platforms/). Users report it "misses a LOT of stuff I watch. What's the point if it misses a bunch of stuff???" (https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/popular-show-tracking-app-tv-time-shutting-down-on-july-15.2484864/), inconsistent timestamps between Netflix and Prime, 2-second plays logged as watched, "Only Netflix and Amazon appear to be syncing correctly. Hulu and Apple tried to sync once but haven't synced since" (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/automatically-sync-your-streaming-services/34947?page=9), and on the roadmap thread the scrobbler "is a complete disaster" (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/public-feedback-board-and-roadmap/59600).

Social is Trakt's blind spot by design: "Trakt has basic follow features, but community is not what it's for" (https://www.achriom.com/blog/letterboxd-vs-trakt/). Years-old requests remain open: react/comment on friends' activity (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/react-interact-with-friends/17485), "Friends who watched this" on title pages (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/friends-who-watched-this-on-movies-shows/88275), seeing what followed users are watching (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/is-there-a-way-to-see-what-people-we-are-following-are-watching/115198). Household tracking is unsolved: "Trakt is one connection/account at a time. If you both watch the same episode it will track first watched and a rewatch but not who" (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/track-multiple-users-watching-shows/111656). Statuses were also weak until Apr 2025's "Drop Show" (removes from Up Next/Progress/Calendar, keeps history) after years of "paused/dropped like AniList" requests (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/adding-a-paused-dropped-feature/20325 ; https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/4/trakt-tv-launches-drop-show-for-easier-watchlist-cleanup-across-all-platforms/). Data export: JSON zip free, CSV VIP-only (https://www.achriom.com/blog/how-to-export-your-trakt-data/). Year in Review launched Dec 2025 (https://x.com/trakt/status/2001765709517377840).

### 2.2 TV Time (2011–2026) and its aftermath
TVShow Time launched in Paris in May 2011, was acquired by Whip Networks (2016), rebranded TV Time (2017), bought TheTVDB (2019) and became a data business (TVLytics for studios; UTA licensed its analytics) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_Time). By 2026 it had ~25M registered users, 26.4M lifetime installs and was still adding ~29k downloads/month when Whip Media — acquired by Blue Torch Capital in early 2025 and pivoting to an enterprise-AI product ("Helix") — announced on July 1 that it would shut on July 15 and delete all personal data: "it was no longer sustainable to continue operating the service as a free app, and there was not enough demand for a paid app" (https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/popular-tv-tracking-app-tv-time-is-shutting-down-as-company-focuses-on-ai/). Co-founder Antonio Pinto later revealed premium subscriptions covered only ~10% of operating costs (https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/13/as-tv-tracking-app-tv-time-shuts-down-its-founder-builds-bingers-a-new-home-for-fans/).

What people loved was not the tracker; it was the ritual. TV Time's community feed only showed comments and memes for episodes you had already marked watched — "unless a user has marked the episode as watched, this content would remain hidden" — with 6,000 memes/week, per-episode emoji reactions, character votes and video reactions (https://techcrunch.com/2015/07/15/tvshow-time-releases-sleek-new-app-for-its-community-of-tv-addicts ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_Time). Pinto: "Reading the community reactions after each episode became a ritual for me, and for many others." Trustpilot post-mortems: "The community was the best part." (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tvtime.com). 25,000+ signed a petition to save it.

What people hated: chronic bugs and data loss ("lost all the shows what i had watched"), a broken web app, search that stopped working, UI churn, and a premium tier that changed underneath early buyers who felt "screwed out of features" (https://justuseapp.com/en/app/431065232/tv-time-track-shows-movies/reviews ; https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tvtime.com). A MacRumors commenter: "They had too much tech debt and weren't keeping pace with other apps." Per-episode ratings never existed ("Per-episode ratings are not part of the design", https://www.achriom.com/blog/serializd-vs-tv-time/). Recommendations "feel commercially driven rather than personalized" (https://www.achriom.com/blog/best-trakt-alternatives/).

The exodus produced the clearest market signal in years. Export was GDPR-request-only, so the community built "TV Time Out" and "TV Time Liberator" (exports with IMDb IDs) to skip the queue (https://www.resetera.com/threads/tv-time-is-shutting-down-july-15th.1566721/). Serializd, Simkl and Refract buckled under traffic; Trakt "doesn't crash". Every competitor published a TV Time landing page and importer within days (Serializd https://www.serializd.com/tvtime ; Hobi; Simkl ZIP importer; Sequel; Refract; Kino). Two successors are trying to rebuild the community piece: Bingers (Pinto; import + episode discussion + cheaper architecture; waitlist as of Aug 2026) and Refract (Florent & Jeremy; free forever, no ads, full export, 16k-member Discord voting on the roadmap, 282 achievements; https://getrefract.app/). Moviebase's framing: "the lesson of this shutdown is that a tracker with no export path can strand you" (https://moviebase.app/resources/tv-time-shutting-down).

### 2.3 Serializd
Serializd is the closest thing to "Letterboxd for TV": rate any show, season or episode on half stars, keep a timestamped diary, write reviews, build lists, follow people, @mention in discussions, see stats (including rewatch time — logging Season 1 again doubles its runtime in your totals), and a where-to-watch panel. Posts carry rating, spoiler tag, rewatch flag and a specific episode (https://www.serializd.com/ ; https://www.achriom.com/blog/best-trakt-alternatives/). It is free, ad-free, no tracking, on web/iOS/Android, imports TV Time and Trakt, and reports 10k+ daily active users. It is built and moderated by one person, whom users describe as "super responsive to feedback" (https://www.producthunt.com/products/serializd).

The community tone is the differentiator: "smaller but more review-focused" where "users write proper reviews that others read", with "fandom twitter/tumblr" vibes (https://www.achriom.com/blog/serializd-vs-tv-time/ ; https://alternativeto.net/software/serializd/about/). That is also its ceiling: TV-only ("requires second app for movies"), "no push notifications. Serializd does not alert you when Severance or The White Lotus drops a new episode", no calendar/reminders, "no built-in export", discovery "basically non-existent", limited sort/filter (maker: "I'm actively working on more extensive sorting and filtering options :)"), occasional missing episodes with slow database fixes, and a web-first stack that strained when TV Time users arrived (https://hobiapp.com/blog/best-tv-show-tracker-apps ; https://www.producthunt.com/products/serializd ; https://www.resetera.com/threads/tv-time-is-shutting-down-july-15th.1566721/).

Serializd proves the demand for a TV journal with taste and a civil community, and that a solo, free product can hold it — but it also shows the gap: a product that unites movies and TV, adds notifications and export, and keeps the diary/spoiler/rewatch grammar would take its users the moment it exists. The most-repeated wish in TV Time threads was literally "what I _really_ want is for Letterboxd to do a TV version of their movie site/app" (https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/popular-show-tracking-app-tv-time-shutting-down-on-july-15.2484864/), and Letterboxd's help center still says returning TV shows are "a future platform extension" (updated June 2, 2026; https://letterboxd.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15269096507407-Do-you-support-TV-shows).

### 2.4 Simkl
Simkl is the free, all-in-one tracker for TV, anime and movies with the broadest automatic capture: a Chrome extension that logs Netflix/Crunchyroll/etc., Plex/Kodi/Emby scrobbling, MAL 2-way sync (VIP), calendars, and since May 2024 watchlists and custom lists (https://alternativeto.net/news/2024/5/simkl-now-has-watchlists-and-custom-lists ; https://simkl.com/). Post-TV Time it shipped a direct ZIP importer and was called "by far the best tracking site" and "SIMKL + SofaTime = best watchlist combo" by refugees (https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/popular-show-tracking-app-tv-time-shutting-down-on-july-15.2484864/).

Its tiering is unusual and instructive. Free: ads, 0 custom lists, no private profile, no CSV backup. PRO (30 lists, private profile, "Watch with friends", quick-edit, CSV) is granted free to anyone who uses Simkl 20+ days a month — an engagement-for-features loop. VIP adds 1,000 lists, automatic JSON backups, MAL sync, iCal and RSS/JSON/CSV export, plus lifetime and student plans (https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/account-and-billing/subscription-plans/features-comparison ; https://simkl.com/vip/). Paywalling a private profile and export is the part users will resent if they notice.

Complaints: "web first" design "misaligned with daily tracking", undocumented TV Time import fidelity, "the social layer is lighter than Trakt's", thinner discovery, and server capacity (https://hobiapp.com/blog/best-tv-show-tracker-apps ; https://www.achriom.com/blog/best-trakt-alternatives/). Simkl is the best evidence that (a) free automatic capture across TV/anime/movies is feasible without VIP gating, and (b) a tracker without a compelling social/diary layer stays a utility people leave when a nicer client appears.

### 2.5 Goodreads vs StoryGraph (what books teach about journaling and challenges)
Goodreads (150M members, Amazon since 2013) shows the power and the rot of a big journaling network. The annual Reading Challenge is a retention machine — "I love reading, but I also love the feeling of people thinking I'm well read" — but the open social surface enables review bombing before publication (Elizabeth Gilbert's *The Snow Forest* was withdrawn), author harassment and extortion, and the interface is "largely unchanged from the 2013 Amazon acquisition": whole-star ratings only, sluggish search, a broken challenge widget, and DNF added only in March 2026 by converting people's "dnf/abandoned/dropped" shelves (https://techxplore.com/news/2025-02-amazon-goodreads-community-division-rival.html ; https://www.achriom.com/blog/storygraph-vs-goodreads/).

StoryGraph (5M+ users Jan 2026, 2025 Apple App Store Award: "In a year when the book community felt increasingly corporatized, StoryGraph built an inclusive space rooted in … authenticity") wins switchers with quarter-star ratings, mood/pace/content-warning tags, built-in DNF and "owned" statuses, deep stats (monthly wrap-ups, year-over-year on Plus $4.99/mo), page/minute/prompt-based challenges, Buddy Reads and Readalongs, Kobo auto-sync (June 2026), and a deliberate absence of comment sections on reviews: founder Nadia Odunayo built it so you don't have "to deal with being scared to put reviews up there" (same sources; https://theboar.org/2025/03/the-storygraph-vs-goodreads-why-you-should-make-the-switch/ ; https://www.readunwritten.com/2026/05/27/4-reasons-to-choose-the-storygraph-over-goodreads/).

Fable adds the club layer Goodreads groups lost: clubs run by authors/celebrities/TikTok creators or yourself, reading streaks, "shelfies", curated "Fables" lists, highlight sharing, and imports from Goodreads and Kindle — "much easier to find an active group than on Goodreads" (https://bookriot.com/fable-book-club-app-review/). Lessons: granular ratings and DNF are table stakes; challenges and stats retain; comment-free or invite-only social prevents the Goodreads failure mode; clubs need a host and a cadence.

### 2.6 Douban — why it works
Douban (founded 2005 by ex-IBM researcher Yang Bo) is "IMDb, Goodreads and Reddit rolled into one", China's "spiritual corner" (精神角落) for the wenyi qingnian ("artistic youth") of first- and second-tier cities (https://www.scmp.com/abacus/who-what/what/article/3100865/how-douban-went-chinas-imdb-its-spiritual-corner ; https://www.theworldofchinese.com/2018/06/killer-app/). Every book, film and album has the same three-state grammar — 想看/在看/看过 (want / doing / done) — plus a short comment (短评) that others vote "useful" (有用), optional long reviews, "Dou-lists" (豆列) for curation, and a "broadcast" timeline of what people you follow marked (https://chozan.co/comprehensive-overview-douban/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douban). Groups (小组, 300,000 by 2012) are interest forums from film to "sewing machines"; city events, a diary, FM radio and a marketplace hang off the same identity.

Why it works: (1) the marking gesture is tiny and universal across media, so a profile becomes an honest cultural fingerprint; (2) Yang designed it "to connect people with things they like or may like … strangers sharing interests rather than existing friends" — "a social network, but for discovering interesting things in life, rather than meeting friends"; (3) it refused the growth playbook: a "slow company", no user classification or membership tiers, pastel white UI, few ads, no celebrity accounts, anonymous handles; (4) ratings are trusted — "There are far fewer shills on Douban than other sites" — so much that Douban scores correlate with domestic box office more strongly than IMDb; (5) groups turn taste into belonging. Costs: a decade of "cautious" monetization (Douban Time paid courses 2017, brand stations, e-commerce), repeated censorship incidents (2009, 2011, 2021 Oscars), and drift — ~60M MAU in 2024 vs 300M peak claims, a departing user: "People no longer express themselves and communicate sincerely." The transferable core: one gesture, cross-media, trust-first ratings, and communities organized around taste rather than existing friend graphs.

### 2.7 Strava-style social fitness
Strava is the reference for making a private log social without making it toxic. Kudos — 14B in 2025, +20% YoY — is a positive-only reaction: "Strava only allows reactions with positive Kudos, and users report … much less negativity than other social platforms" (https://www.strivecloud.io/blog/app-engagement-strava). Visibility to people whose opinion matters makes people "not always more ambitious, but more consistent"; users open the app 35+ times/month vs <15 for competitors; group activities get 95–121% more kudos than solo (same). The mechanics are deliberately segmented: segments create "thousands of hyper-local, context-specific rankings" so average users can hold a title; KOM (fastest) and Local Legend (most frequent in 90 days) run in parallel so the system doesn't "lose everyone else"; challenges are time-boxed; clubs give "group context … without requiring real-world coordination"; streaks are weekly because "rest and recovery are part of the programme". Retention: users completing hardest achievements retain at 74.2% vs 32.3% for easiest; repeating leaderboards drive 3.4× more engagement than perpetual ones (https://trophy.so/blog/strava-gamification-case-study). Beli applies the same ideas to restaurants: pairwise ranking instead of stars, a "friend rating" per place, predicted scores for you and your friends, and a feed "full of familiar faces" because only followed users appear (https://www.today.com/food/trends/what-is-beli-app-rcna217748). Untappd's badges "are often the main reason casual users return" (https://lounge.untappd.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-untappd-badges/). For a watch journal: positive-only reactions, local/friend-scoped leaderboards ("most episodes of X among your friends"), episodic challenges, weekly not daily streaks, and clubs around a show.

### 2.8 Last.fm / Airbuds (passive capture and passive sharing)
Last.fm has survived since 2002 on one idea: scrobble automatically, own the history, and periodically hand it back as identity — its "Playback" year-in-review ships in early January, on the web, and is praised precisely for not being premature like Wrapped (https://tantek.com/2025/003/t1/lastfm-year-in-review-playback24 ; https://lukealexdavis.co.uk/posts/last-fm-playback-2025/). Airbuds (15M downloads, 5M MAU, 1.5M DAU, 96% positive review sentiment, $10M raised) is the modern version: a home-screen widget showing what your close friends are playing right now across Spotify/Apple Music/etc., with emoji/sticker/selfie reactions, a weekly recap, taste-match suggestions and "ghost mode". Co-founder Gilles Poupardin: "when you ask the users to create a playlist or to do something, it's a lot of effort … it's effortless. You just connect your Spotify"; ~30% of users go beyond viewing to react; "The streamers gave us access to 100 million songs, but nobody really cracked the identity piece, the self-expression piece" (https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/17/airbuds-is-the-music-social-network-apple-and-spotify-wish-they-had-built/). Apple's Ping/Connect and Spotify's social attempts failed because they were built on the catalog, not the person. Video's equivalent capture path is harder (streamers have no public APIs; Trakt's scrobbler is 24-hour, VIP-only and lossy; browser extensions break), which is why manual "one-tap episode marking" remains the bar every tracker is judged by — and why a widget-first "what my friends are watching right now" surface is still unclaimed for film/TV.

## 4. "Nobody has solved this well" — ranked open gaps

1. **Household / "watched with" progress.** Every tracker models one person per account. Trakt: "If you both watch the same episode it will track first watched and a rewatch but not who … use separate devices … not ideal" (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/track-multiple-users-watching-shows/111656). Moviebase's couples guide is a workaround manual: two accounts + a shared list, or one account and lose your stats; symptoms are "where did we stop?", a partner watching ahead, a watchlist that becomes "a battlefield", "spend the first 30 minutes of movie night arguing", accidental spoilers (https://moviebase.app/resources/how-to-track-movies-and-shows-as-a-couple). Simkl's PRO "Watch with friends" is the only hint of a native model and it is paywalled and undocumented.
2. **Movies + TV + a real community in one product.** Letterboxd is film-only and has said "we're working on it" for TV since 2023 (https://letterboxd.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15269096507407-Do-you-support-TV-shows); Serializd is TV-only; Trakt has both but "community is not what it's for"; TV Time had community but no per-episode ratings and is gone. The single most-repeated wish: "what I _really_ want is for Letterboxd to do a TV version" (MacRumors); "You can't review TV shows here" (Letterboxd Trustpilot).
3. **Spoiler-safe, progress-gated episode discussion at scale.** TV Time's gating ("unless a user has marked the episode as watched, this content would remain hidden") created the ritual 25M people miss; no survivor has it at scale. Bingers (waitlist) and Phictly (auto-blurs spoilers until you're ready) are attempts (https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/13/... ; https://www.digitaltrends.com/social-media/check-this-new-social-app-which-lets-you-spoil-your-favourite-tv-shows-and-books/).
4. **Reliable, free automatic capture from streaming apps.** Trakt's scrobbler is VIP-only, daily, and "misses a LOT"; the free extension has open bugs since 2023 (https://github.com/trakt-tools/universal-trakt-scrobbler/issues/329); Apple's Up Next "stopped properly tracking … I have to manually mark episodes" (https://discussions.apple.com/thread/255800778) and omits Netflix. Media-server scrobbling works but only for the Plex minority.
5. **Deciding what to watch together, as a feature not an app.** A graveyard of swipe apps (CineSwipe, Movie Swiper, MoviePals, Matched, Binger, WatchPick, Netflip…) solve one night and retain nothing; Bingie's App Store listing died at v2.0.2, Dec 2021, 2.1★ (https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/bingie-movies-tv-shows/id1511254273); "Shared Watchlist: Couple match" charges a subscription just to invite a partner. Reelgood's "Swipe With Friends" faded with its consumer app. The need is real (Cinesift's harmonized-score+availability filters drew film buffs in 2016; https://www.vice.com/en/article/cinesift-movie-review-site-netflix-amazon-prime/); nobody has fused it with a persistent shared list and taste overlap ("For Us" in WeWatch is the closest).
6. **Friend recommendations that don't get lost.** Bingie's thesis ("your friends … can give you the best recommendations. It's not about an algorithm", https://dot.la/bingie-streaming-guide-app-2647091799.html) and WatchMates' "never forget what someone told you to watch" (https://www.watchmates.app/) are right; Beli's "friend rating" proves it works in food. Trakt users have begged for "Friends who watched this" for years.
7. **First-class statuses: paused, dropped, DNF, "caught up".** Trakt only got Drop Show in 2025 and V3 hid the dropped list ("Where did the dropped shows list/option go?"); Letterboxd has no DNF; Goodreads took 13 years. AniList, Backloggd (Abandoned/Retired/Shelved), StoryGraph and Hobi's six criteria ("real status options: Watching, On Hold, Dropped, Plan to Watch") show the bar.
8. **Portability and trust.** TV Time deleted 25M histories; Trakt sells CSV; Simkl sells export and even a private profile; JustWatch: "They dont provide your watchlist export"; IMDb users ask "Is there going to be a way to export the entries from the Watch History?" Refugees' rule: "look for apps that provide easy watchlist export features!"
9. **Privacy-by-default social.** Letterboxd has no private accounts (Feb 2026: "in the works") and will never add DMs because they "would be a moderation and privacy nightmare"; Plex's forced privacy choice and StoryGraph's no-comment reviews are the good patterns; Airbuds' ghost mode is the expectation.
10. **Non-hostile monetization.** Trakt's 2025 hikes produced 89% one-star Trustpilot; TV Time's premium covered 10% of costs; Letterboxd (<30 staff, $19/$49 tiers "without fundamentally altering the free tier") and Callsheet ($9/yr) and Backloggd (Patreon $3) show what works.
11. **"When does it come back?" surfaced where you look.** Ex-TV Time user to Trakt: "relied on TV Time to show me the premieres of the first episodes in my watch next section"; Serializd has no notifications; Apple TV removes a caught-up show instead of parking it: "i want the shows i've selected to my Watchlist to remain … and not disappear unless am doing the clicking" (https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256264658).
12. **Stats that feel like a diary.** Trakt V3 deleted daily watch-time stats and the progress page users called "most essential"; the roadmap's top asks are stats/history restorations; Letterboxd gates stats behind Pro; Serializd's rewatch-doubling and Backloggd's journal calendar ("makes your gaming year legible") are the model; Trakt users asked for "Month in review" years ago (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/month-in-review-movie-stats/48379).

## 6. Twelve lessons for a new movie+TV journal/social platform

1. **Own the data layer and be exportable from day one.** TV Time deleted 25M histories and its refugees' first rule was "look for apps that provide easy watchlist export"; Trakt sells CSV, Simkl sells export, JustWatch has none. Free JSON/CSV export, plus importers for Trakt/Letterboxd/TV Time/IMDb/Simkl, is now a trust signal, not a feature. (https://moviebase.app/resources/tv-time-shutting-down ; https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/account-and-billing/subscription-plans/features-comparison)
2. **Movies and TV in one diary, at episode granularity, with per-episode ratings and season reviews.** The loudest wish is a "Letterboxd for TV" that Letterboxd hasn't shipped in three years; Serializd shows per-episode half-stars + season reviews + rewatch flags work; TV Time died without per-episode ratings. (https://letterboxd.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15269096507407-Do-you-support-TV-shows ; https://www.achriom.com/blog/serializd-vs-tv-time/)
3. **Passive capture first, expression on top — but make the manual path one tap.** Airbuds: "when you ask the users … to do something, it's a lot of effort"; Last.fm has lived on scrobbles for 24 years. Streaming APIs are closed, so ship media-server scrobbling (Trakt/Plex/Jellyfin), a browser extension, and the best one-tap episode marking with countdowns — Hobi's six criteria are the acceptance test. (https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/17/... ; https://hobiapp.com/blog/best-tv-show-tracker-apps)
4. **Model *who* watched, not just *what*.** Couples and households are unserved: "it will track first watched and a rewatch but not who." A "watched with" field, per-person progress on a shared show, and a shared-but-separate watchlist would be a unique wedge. (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/track-multiple-users-watching-shows/111656 ; https://moviebase.app/resources/how-to-track-movies-and-shows-as-a-couple)
5. **Gate discussion by progress; spoilers are the social unlock.** TV Time's whole community ran on "hidden unless you've marked the episode watched" and it is the thing people mourn. Spoiler tags (Serializd, Letterboxd, Backloggd) are the floor; progress-gated threads per episode are the ceiling. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_Time ; https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/13/...)
6. **A feed of people you chose, with positive-only reactions, and no open comment war.** Beli's feed is "full of familiar faces"; Strava's kudos are positive-only and users report "much less negativity"; StoryGraph deliberately has no comments on reviews; Goodreads' open surface "breeds division". Kudos + close-friends by default; comments opt-in per post. (https://www.today.com/food/trends/what-is-beli-app-rcna217748 ; https://www.strivecloud.io/blog/app-engagement-strava ; https://techxplore.com/news/2025-02-amazon-goodreads-community-division-rival.html)
7. **First-class statuses: watching, paused, dropped/DNF, caught-up, rewatching.** AniList/Backloggd/StoryGraph have them; Trakt only got "Drop Show" in 2025 after years of requests and Letterboxd has none. Preserve history when dropping. (https://forums.trakt.tv/t/adding-a-paused-dropped-feature/20325 ; https://backloggd.com/about/)
8. **Never remove things from the user's watchlist on their behalf; answer "when does it come back?" where they look.** Apple TV's auto-removal of caught-up shows is universally hated; ex-TV Time users want premieres surfaced in "watch next"; Serializd's lack of notifications is its top complaint. Park caught-up shows in a "waiting" state with a return date. (https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256264658 ; https://forums.trakt.tv/t/feature-request-suggestion-from-a-former-tv-time-user/115309)
9. **Where-to-watch filtered by the user's own subscriptions, but never as the product.** Trakt's free "Watch now" filter and Sequel's JustWatch integration are praised; JustWatch itself is "wrong 85% of the time" and monetizes by nagging. Use it as a filter on your list and on "decide together", not as the reason to open the app. (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.justwatch.com ; https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/...2484864/)
10. **Stats and a year-in-review are retention, and they must be a diary, not a spreadsheet.** Trakt's V3 removed daily stats and its progress page, and the roadmap is full of restore-my-stats votes; Letterboxd's Year in Review needs 10 logged films and Pro gets stats year-round; Last.fm's Playback is loved for shipping in January on the web; Untappd's badges bring people back in December. Monthly wrap-ups, watch-time, rewatch-time, streaks (weekly, à la Strava). (https://roadmap.trakt.tv/en ; https://letterboxd.com/journal/2025-letterboxd-year-in-review-faq/ ; https://trophy.so/blog/strava-gamification-case-study)

12. **"Decide together" is a mode on top of the shared list and taste overlap — not a swipe app.** Standalone swipe apps have no retention (Bingie last updated Dec 2021, 2.1★; dozens of clones charging to pair).
